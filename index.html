Featured Research

Pancreatic cancer biomarker identification

Undergraduate Thesis · 2025–2026

Biomarker Identification in Pancreatic Cancer Using Two-Step Feature Selection and WGCNA

An integrated study combining gene-expression analysis, two-stage feature selection, machine-learning and deep-learning classifiers, WGCNA, protein–protein interaction analysis, and biological validation to investigate potential pancreatic-cancer biomarkers.

Supervisor: Utsha Das, Assistant Professor, Department of CSE, RUET

FocusComputational biomarker discovery
DataGene-expression datasets
EvaluationAccuracy, AUC, Precision, Recall, F1
Gene Expression→DEG→mRMR→PLS-DA→ML / DL→WGCNA→PPI→Biological Validation
Research question

Can complementary feature-selection, co-expression, and network-analysis methods narrow high-dimensional pancreatic-cancer gene-expression data into a smaller set of biologically meaningful biomarker candidates?

My contribution

Designed and executed an integrated computational workflow covering preprocessing, differential-expression analysis, two-stage feature selection, classifier evaluation, WGCNA, PPI analysis, and downstream biological interpretation.

Candidate hub genes

NUSAP1, ANLN, MELK, KIF14, NUF2, and TPX2 emerged as the final hub-gene panel investigated in the study. These are computational candidates and require further independent and experimental validation.

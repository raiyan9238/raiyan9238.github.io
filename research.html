Research

My current research direction focuses on computational approaches for biomedical data analysis, particularly gene-expression analysis, machine-learning-based feature selection, and biomarker discovery.

2025–2026
Undergraduate Thesis

Biomarker Identification in Pancreatic Cancer Using Two-Step Feature Selection and WGCNA

Supervisor: Utsha Das, Assistant Professor, Department of CSE, RUET

The thesis develops an integrated computational workflow to investigate potential pancreatic-cancer biomarkers from gene-expression data. It combines statistical differential-expression analysis with feature selection, predictive modeling, co-expression network analysis, PPI-network analysis, and downstream biological interpretation.

Research question

Can complementary feature-selection, predictive-modeling, co-expression, and network-analysis methods reduce high-dimensional pancreatic-cancer gene-expression data into a smaller and biologically meaningful set of candidate biomarkers?

Methodology

Gene Expression→DEG→mRMR→PLS-DA→ML / DL→WGCNA→PPI→Biological Validation

My contribution

I designed and executed the integrated analysis workflow, including preprocessing, differential-expression analysis, two-stage feature selection, classifier evaluation, WGCNA, PPI analysis, and downstream biological interpretation.

Final computational candidate panel

NUSAP1, ANLN, MELK, KIF14, NUF2, and TPX2 emerged as the final hub-gene panel investigated in the thesis. These are computational biomarker candidates and require independent and experimental validation before clinical interpretation.
